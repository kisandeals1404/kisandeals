Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 22, 2026  ·  Applies to the KisanDeals website (kisandeals.com) and the KisanDeals mobile app

1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy is published by KisanDeals (OPC) Private Limited ("KisanDeals", "we", "us") and describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the KisanDeals website or the KisanDeals mobile app (Android/iOS). By using either, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

  • Mobile number: used to log you in via a one-time password (OTP). This is the only required signup information.
  • Profile details (optional): name, email address, and account type (e.g. Farmer, Trader), if you choose to complete your profile.
  • Listings and business content: anything you post — crop/livestock/equipment/land listings, business profiles, descriptions, prices, and photos you upload.
  • Location: only if you grant permission (on the app, for weather and nearest-mandi/city features). You can deny or revoke this anytime in your device settings.
  • Payment information: when you subscribe to a paid plan or promote a listing/business, payment is processed entirely by Razorpay, our payment gateway partner. We never see or store your card, UPI, or net-banking credentials — we only receive confirmation that a payment succeeded or failed.
  • Push notification token (app only): a device-specific token (via Expo's push service) used to send you mandi/egg price update notifications, along with your selected app language and platform (Android/iOS). This token is not linked to your identity unless you are logged in.
  • Usage data: standard web/app logs (IP address, pages viewed, approximate timestamps) used for security, abuse prevention, and improving the service.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To verify your identity via OTP and keep your account secure.
  • To show your listings/business to other users and let interested buyers/sellers contact you.
  • To process subscription and promotion payments and activate the plan you purchased.
  • To send price-update push notifications and OTP SMS messages you've requested.
  • To show weather and nearby-mandi information based on your location, if permitted.
  • To respond to support requests sent via the Help page or chat assistant.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, spam, and abuse of the platform.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Third-Party Services We Use

We rely on the following service providers, each of which processes only the data needed for its specific function:

  • Razorpay — payment processing for subscriptions and promotions.
  • 2Factor.in — SMS delivery for OTP login codes.
  • ImageKit — hosting and delivery of photos you upload for listings and businesses.
  • Expo (push notification service) — delivering push notifications to the mobile app.
  • Google Translate API — powering dynamic translation of mandi/listing data into Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, and Malayalam.
  • Open-Meteo — weather forecast data, fetched using only your approximate location (no account data is shared).
  • MongoDB Atlas — our database provider, which stores the data described in Section 2 on our behalf.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and listing data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account or ask us to, we remove your personal information within 30 days, except where we're required to retain payment records for legal/tax purposes (typically up to the period required by Indian law). Inactive push notification tokens are automatically removed once a device stops accepting notifications.

6. Your Choices & Rights

  • You can edit or remove your profile, listings, and business pages anytime from your account.
  • You can turn off location or notification permissions anytime in your device settings.
  • You can request a copy of your data, or ask us to delete your account and associated data, by emailing support@kisandeals.com.
  • You can log out anytime, which ends your session on that device.

7. Children's Privacy

KisanDeals is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us information, contact us and we will remove it.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and access controls on our database and admin systems. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your data using reasonable technical and organizational safeguards.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the website or app after a change means you accept the revised policy.

Questions about this policy or your data? Contact us at support@kisandeals.com, or see our Terms & Conditions and Help page.
